Account and data requests

Data Deletion

If you use a DGTech Lab website, service, or application and want to request deletion of personal information associated with your account or use of the service, you may contact us by email.

How to Request Deletion

Email admin@dgtechlab.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include enough information for us to identify the relevant account, app, project, or service.

Verification

We may need to verify your identity or authority before processing a deletion request. This helps protect accounts and business data from unauthorized changes.

What May Be Deleted

Depending on the service, deletion may include account profile information, submitted contact information, app usage records, uploaded files, or other personal information associated with your request.

What May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, backup, accounting, contractual, or legitimate business purposes.

Contact

For data deletion requests, contact admin@dgtechlab.com.
